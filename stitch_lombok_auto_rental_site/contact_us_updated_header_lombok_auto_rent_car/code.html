Frequently Asked Questions

Can I book a car for airport pickup? expand_more

Yes, we provide seamless airport transfers. Simply provide your flight number and our driver will wait with a sign in the arrivals hall.

What documents are required for rental? expand_more

For domestic rentals, an ID (KTP) and driving license (SIM A). For international travelers, we require a Passport and International Driving Permit.

Do you offer 24/7 breakdown support? expand_more

Absolutely. Our emergency roadside assistance is available round-the-clock across all areas of Lombok.
