The Ultimate Guide to Exploring South Lombok's Secret Beaches with a Car Rental

While the Gili Islands often steal the spotlight, the true soul of Lombok lies in its rugged southern coastline. Here, the roads wind through lime-green tobacco fields and open up to panoramic vistas of the Indian Ocean.

1. Driving to Mawun Beach: The Horseshoe Paradise

Mawun is a masterpiece of coastal geography. Almost perfectly circular, this bay is shielded by two massive headlands that keep the waters calm and turquoise. It's the perfect spot for a morning swim before the tropical sun reaches its peak.

Driving to Mawun requires a vehicle with reliable suspension and responsive handling.

2. Selong Belanak: A Kuta Lombok Road Trip Highlight

Widely regarded as one of the best beaches in Asia for beginner surfers, Selong Belanak features a wide, flat stretch of pristine white sand. The waves here break gently over a sandy bottom, making it a safe haven for those looking to catch their first wave.

lightbulb Pro Tip for Adventurers

  • check_circle Arrive before 9:00 AM to see the famous buffalo parade across the beach.
  • check_circle Hire a local instructor right on the sand for roughly 250k IDR.
  • check_circle Park your rental car in the designated area to keep the shoreline pristine.
