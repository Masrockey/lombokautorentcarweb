02

Rental Eligibility & Verification

Age Requirement

Drivers must be at least 21 years of age. For premium luxury fleet models, the minimum age requirement is 25.

Licensing

A valid International Driving Permit (IDP) or Indonesian SIM is mandatory for foreign nationals. Domestic travelers must present a valid SIM A.

The original physical license must be presented at the time of vehicle handover. Photocopies or digital versions without original verification are not accepted.
